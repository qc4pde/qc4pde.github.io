# QC4PDE Consortium: Executive Summary

The QC4PDE Consortium is a collaborative, pre-competitive initiative focused on
determining whether quantum computing can provide practical value for PDE and
CFD applications.

It is founded on the recognition that there is currently no proven case for
quantum utility, no dominant method, and no clear hardware path in this area.
Its role is therefore not to advocate for quantum computing, but to evaluate
quantum, classical, and hybrid approaches rigorously and objectively.

The Consortium’s mission is to:

- identify relevant scientific and industrial use cases,
- define and maintain meaningful benchmarks,
- assess methods fairly across full workflows,
- support credible resource estimation and roadmap analysis,
- and share methods, code, metadata, and results as openly as possible.

QC4PDE is guided by principles of scientific relevance, outcome neutrality,
collaboration, vendor neutrality, openness, and FAIR data practices. It
emphasizes evaluating complete systems and workflows, not just isolated
algorithms.

Its work will span:

- foundational benchmark problems,
- progressively harder stretch-goal cases,
- real-world application scenarios,
- validation, performance, and resource-assessment methods.

The Consortium will be governed by a Steering Committee with specialized
Working Groups and is expected to produce benchmark suites, open tools,
technical studies, metadata standards, roadmap assessments, and community
resources.

**In short:** QC4PDE exists to generate credible evidence and practical
guidance on whether, where, and under what conditions quantum computing may be
useful for PDE/CFD—including concluding when it is not.
