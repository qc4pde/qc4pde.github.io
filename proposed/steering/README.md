The co-chairs of the Steering & Outreach Committee also serve as the co-chairs for the QC4PDE consortium at large. As activities may require, further sub-committees of the Steering & Outreach committee may form and have their own chairs. 


## Purpose 

The purpose of the Steering Committee is to coordinate between the overall activities of the consortium, initiate meetings and other gatherings, and to document and promote the activities of the group. 

Activities may include but are not limited to:

- organizing periodic all-consortium meetings and keeping minutes

- initiating sub-teams to coordinate in-person gatherings or other special events (e.g. IEEE workshop)

- presenting topical invited talks  

- coordinating broader collaborations with similar-to groups in academia, industry, and government

- campus and student outreach 

- maintaining calendars of group interest, such as: call for publication dates, important conferences, RFPs

- supporting consortium members with funding calls, and awareness thereof 

- promoting the work of the consortium and its members via member lists, publication reference lists, media announcements, and other promotional mechanisms for the benefit of the consortium
