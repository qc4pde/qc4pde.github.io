# QC4PDE Meetings

The full consortium meets monthly, generally but not always on the 2nd Friday
of the month at 11am Eastern Time. Each committee also runs its own working
meetings, open to anyone; see the [committee pages](../committees/index.md).

**Meeting link:** [get the Zoom link](join.html) (complete a quick CAPTCHA)
