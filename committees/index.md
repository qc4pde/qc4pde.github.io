# QC4PDE Committees

The consortium's work is organized into three committees, each led by co-chairs.
Members can take part in any number of committees, and each committee runs its
own working meetings, open to anyone. See the [charter](../charter/index.md) for
the consortium's mission and principles.

## [Steering & Outreach](steering/index.md)

Coordinates the consortium as a whole: all-consortium meetings and minutes,
events and workshops, outreach to similar groups, funding calls, and new
members.

## [Benchmarks & Stewardship](benchmarks/index.md)

Defines the benchmarks of relevance to the consortium, standards for
publication and comparability, and a catalog of results. Owns the data.

## [Software & Tooling](software/index.md)

Supports research with portable, reusable frameworks, metadata tooling, and
repository infrastructure. Supports the data, but does not own it.
